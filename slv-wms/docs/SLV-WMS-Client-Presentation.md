# SLV WMS
### Warehouse Management System for SLV Group Sdn. Bhd.

A single system to track every unit of stock across every warehouse — received, stored, picked, invoiced and delivered — with a phone as the only tool your floor staff need.

Prepared for: SLV Group management
Status: Core system live and ready to deploy

---

## The problem we set out to solve

- Stock spread across multiple warehouses with no single source of truth
- Inventory value is a guess, not a number — costing drifts over time
- Receiving, picking and shipping run on paper, WhatsApp and memory
- No audit trail when stock goes missing
- Staff type item codes by hand — slow and error-prone

**The cost:** lost stock, wrong stock value on the books, slow fulfilment, no accountability.

---

## What SLV WMS is

A production-grade warehouse system built around four principles:

- **Multi-warehouse from day one** — every transaction knows which warehouse it belongs to
- **True cost valuation** — inventory value is calculated, not estimated, down to each receipt
- **Zero-typing on mobile** — staff scan, they don't type
- **Every stock move is logged and reversible** — full audit trail

No new infrastructure. No per-user licensing. No annual lock-in.

---

## The end-to-end flow

**Receive → Putaway → Pick → Pack → Invoice → Deliver**

Each step is a scan on a phone or a click on the desktop. Stock value updates the moment a unit moves. Every step is timestamped against a staff member.

One warehouse or ten — the flow is identical and the numbers stay consistent.

---

## Master data — set it up once

- **Products / SKUs** with multiple barcodes each (retail barcodes, QR, internal labels) — supports ~10,000 items
- **Warehouses → Zones → Racks → Bins** — a precise address for every unit of stock
- **Suppliers & Customers** with billing details and tax numbers
- **Categories & tax groups** — fully configurable
- **Bulk import from a spreadsheet** — bring in thousands of products, bins or opening stock in minutes, with row-by-row error reporting

---

## Mobile barcode scanning — the differentiator

Your staff already carry a phone. That's the whole device.

- Camera scans barcodes and QR codes — receive, putaway, pick
- Audible beep + vibration on every successful scan
- Installs like an app on Android and iPhone — no app store needed
- Double-tap-safe: an accidental double scan can never double-count stock

**Target:** a full receive-to-shelf cycle in under 60 seconds per item.

---

## Accurate stock value

Most small-business systems use a rough average cost. SLV WMS tracks **every receipt as its own cost layer**.

- Inventory value = the real cost of what is physically on the shelf
- Oldest stock is always consumed first
- Drill into any item and see exactly which costs, in which bins
- The number on your dashboard is the number you can put on your books

---

## Goods Receipt with smart putaway

1. Create a goods receipt against a supplier and a warehouse
2. Receive each line — quantity + actual unit cost
3. System **suggests the best bin** — consolidate with existing stock, or the first empty bin in the item's zone
4. Confirm by scan — stock is now live and valued

Status moves automatically through every stage and can never drift out of sync.

---

## Sales orders, picking & tax

- Create a sales order — customer, warehouse, lines with per-line tax
- System **warns if a line exceeds available stock** before you commit
- Generates a pick list with an **optimised walk path** so the picker walks the floor once
- Picker scans bin → item → quantity on the phone; stock is consumed automatically, oldest first
- **Multi-tax handling** — supports SST and stacked/compound taxes per line

---

## Invoicing & Delivery Orders

- Invoice generated from **what was actually picked** — short supplies bill correctly, never for stock that didn't ship
- Per-tax breakdown stored for **SST filing**
- Professional **A4 Invoice and Delivery Order** documents with your logo, your colours, your company details
- Delivery lifecycle: Ready → In-transit → Delivered, with driver + vehicle captured
- The whole chain auto-updates: complete the delivery and the sales order closes itself

---

## Reports & live dashboard

- **Stock on hand** — per item, per warehouse, with full cost valuation and drilldown
- **Stock movement ledger** — every quantity change, filterable by warehouse, item, type and date
- **Role-aware dashboard** — each staff member sees the numbers relevant to their job, filtered to the warehouses they're allowed in
- Real numbers, updated the instant stock moves

---

## Security & accountability

- **8 roles**: super admin, warehouse manager, receiver, picker, packer, driver, sales, viewer
- Staff only see and act in the **warehouses they are explicitly granted**
- **Every stock-changing action is logged** — who, what, when, from where
- Branding, document numbering and tax rules are all configurable — nothing fixed in stone
